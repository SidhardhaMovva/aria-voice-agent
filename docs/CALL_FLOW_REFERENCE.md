# Call Flow Reference

Use this reference when reviewing the end-to-end caller experience for Aria.

## Opening

- Greet the caller briefly after the WebRTC session connects.
- Avoid asking for patient details until a tool flow needs them.
- Let the caller lead with appointment, FAQ, reminder, records, or staff-handoff intent.
- Keep early turns short so latency problems are easy to notice.

## Information Gathering

- Ask one clear follow-up question at a time.
- Reuse known caller details only when the lookup flow supplies them.
- Confirm dates and times before booking, rescheduling, or canceling.
- Avoid collecting sensitive details that are not needed for the current action.

## Tool Results

- Summarize successful tool results in caller-friendly language.
- Include doctor, date, time, and callback details when confirming appointment work.
- For records and reminders, explain the expected staff follow-up path.
- If a tool fails, apologize briefly and offer staff escalation.

## Escalation and Closing

- Escalate medical advice, emergencies, billing disputes, complaints, and explicit requests for a person.
- Do not continue troubleshooting after the caller accepts staff handoff.
- End the call after a clear goodbye or completion phrase.
- Keep the closing message calm and concise.
